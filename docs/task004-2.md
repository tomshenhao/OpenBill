# Task 004.2 — visible results and supplier acquisition

## Working consumer functionality

Region and period inputs are visible in the main bill form. Optional first/last
usage dates are inclusive: valid dates calculate the day count, and an exact calendar
month selects/confirms its archived month automatically. Manual days/month entry
remains supported. Date-derived day count and month cannot be edited inconsistently.
After calculation, focus moves to the results. A regional summary appears with the
bill metrics; the full bar chart and middle-50% classification follow the bill
breakdown, before supplier availability and saving tips. Source details stay expandable.

## Approximate dated references

Nonmatching periods require explicit opt-in. For each covered month, the reference
is `monthly mean kWh × covered calendar days / total days in that month`; contributions
are summed. Both endpoints count. Every covered month and region must exist in the
archive, otherwise no reference is returned. No historical/future values are generated.

This is an approximate reference under a uniform-within-month daily-use assumption,
not a measured same-period household distribution. Weather, occupancy and changing
regional samples can invalidate that assumption. It is never used for tariff matching,
savings, a percentile, or below/within/above quartile classification. Monthly quantiles
are not added/averaged: their joint household distribution is unknown. Exact matches
retain the original source quartiles and provenance gates.
