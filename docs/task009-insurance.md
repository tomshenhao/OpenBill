# Task 009 · Six independent insurance guides

The insurance directory and six guides are available in English and Simplified
Chinese. Each guide explains the product, policy terms and practical ways to
review cover. The source links support general New Zealand information.

The [module registry](../src/config/insurance.ts) records each guide's route and
sources. The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) records the separate Tasks 009.1–009.6 and
their status. The shared component renders guide content. The separate
[house-insurance tool](task009-1-house-insurance.md) keeps manually entered terms
and quotes in browser memory.

## Future comparison evidence

For each product, define comparison criteria and a product-specific model before
collecting evidence. Keep submissions and evidence separate by module. Identify
the provider, policy version, effective date, eligibility, limits and exclusions.
Use official provider sources, preserve permitted evidence locally and have an
independent reviewer confirm the record and its publication rights before
release. General guidance links do not verify provider policies or confer
permission to reproduce them.

Keep quote requests, contact details, policy uploads and medical information
outside OpenBill. For comparisons, use the actual scope of each policy and
surface differences in cover and excess alongside any verified premium.
