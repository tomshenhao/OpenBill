# Task 009.1 · House policy review and quote comparison

The English and Simplified Chinese house routes support manual building-policy
review and compare up to five alternatives against the current policy. Keep
premiums on the same annual basis, including applicable taxes, levies and fees.
Differences are calculated in whole cents. The results show different, missing
and conditional terms before the premium difference. They do not rank policies.
An excess expressed as a percentage or dependent on the event belongs in notes
unless the same event can be compared as a fixed amount.

Enter an insurer and product by name, followed by sum insured, standard excess
and optional annual premium. All five can be skipped. Each has contextual help.
The review button shows the entered values and, when a valid annual premium is
present, monthly and weekly averages based on 12 months and 52 weeks. The
comparison reads the same values as a read-only reference and updates as they
change. Its results include annual and monthly equivalent differences.
Event cards explain general coverage questions and show **Coverage not yet
verified** for the policy named by the user.
Optional event notes show the visitor's own reading only after a wording version
and clause or evidence reference have been entered. Neither the selector nor
the notes verifies a claim. Quote date, hazard excess and other comparison
conditions are in optional Advanced Details. User entries stay in temporary
page state. No uploads, personalised quote requests or insurer coverage
records are used.

Previously checked product-name references from 20 November 2026 include AA Insurance's
[Home Insurance wording](https://www.aainsurance.co.nz/manage-policy/policy-documents/home-insurance-policy-document)
identifies an edition for new and renewing policies starting on or after
1 July 2026. [Tower's house insurance page](https://www.tower.co.nz/house-insurance/)
lists its three cover levels. These examples confirm published names. Users
can type any insurer or product. The app does not apply a wording version or
provider clause based on a typed name.

## Indicative prices pending

- [Treasury / Finity · November 2025 residential insurance study](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2026-02/insurance-data-collection-report-nov25.pdf).
  Background and Context, pp. 6–8, describes October 2025 quotes for 3,510
  profiles, seven websites across four underwriters, geographic groups, rebuild
  sums and building characteristics. Default excesses and cover options differ
  by insurer and the regional summaries aggregate varying quotes. It supports
  market research. The figures are historical and the report asserts Finity
  copyright. Permission to reuse the figures for an OpenBill benchmark is unconfirmed.

  describes the 2025 house-and-contents survey across six centres, including a
  comparison at matched sum insured and excess. The public release does not
  provide a full comparable building-only dataset with exact quote dates,
  product cover, and provider counts per price range. Redistribution permission
  is unconfirmed under the [published terms](https://www.consumer.org.nz/about-us/terms-and-conditions).
- [Reserve Bank · Financial Stability Report, May 2026](https://www.rbnz.govt.nz/-/media/project/sites/rbnz/files/publications/financial-stability-reports/2026/may/financial-stability-report-may-2026.pdf).
  The insurance affordability discussion gives a national annual average for
  domestic buildings. It does not specify like-for-like quote ranges by
  location, rebuild limit, excess and coverage. The average is market context.

To publish a benchmark, document the observation date, geography, property
type, rebuild limit, excess, coverage level, sample size and represented
providers, source and permission for the planned reuse. Keep benchmark and
manual quote results independent.
